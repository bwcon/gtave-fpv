gtavefpv dlcpack: the drones and the pilot's goggles and radio for GTA V Enhanced FPV.

5" quad ("Neon Falcon") and its propeller ("Lime Propeller"): made by bwcon
with Meshy AI (https://www.meshy.ai). The quad's modelled props were cut out
so separate propellers can spin; both were reduced, scaled to a real 5" quad
(226 mm motor diagonal, 127 mm props) and converted with Sollumz.

Model: "DJI Avata2" by raphael.harris.gaffga
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dji-avata2-e27ed758e2174a89a48368e84027f8d9
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: reduced from 200,579 to 15,000 triangles, turned to face GTA's
forward axis, its materials replaced by flat colours, and converted to GTA V
formats (.ydr, .ytd, .ytyp) with Sollumz. The build scripts are in
tools/models of https://github.com/bwcon/gtave-fpv.

Goggles: "Tripo FPV DJI Goggles" by line-o
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tripo-fpv-dji-goggles-9edb8544e5ba4cd1985fd997d366b260
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: reduced from 423,980 to 8,000 triangles, scaled to real size (0.22 m
across), turned to face GTA's forward axis, its colour texture reduced to
512 px and darkened to match the drone's black body, and converted to GTA V
formats with Sollumz.

Radio: "Radiomaster TX12 rc radio controller" by flightclubua
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/radiomaster-tx12-rc-radio-controller-0edc6bca739d4aa6952bb6b8747318bb
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: reduced from 39,646 to 8,000 triangles, scaled to real size (0.175 m
across), its colour texture reduced to 512 px, and converted to GTA V
formats with Sollumz.
